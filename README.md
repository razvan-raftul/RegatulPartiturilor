# Music Box

A Windows program that reads sheet music for blind musicians, fully accessible with NVDA and the keyboard:
in words, measure by measure and hand by hand ("Measure 3. Right hand: E5 eighth note, G5 eighth note..."),
and in Braille music (on screen, or as a BRF file for an embosser).

It also plays the music (with a metronome), lets you play on the computer keyboard like a piano,
write your own scores as text and save them as MusicXML or BRF, and practise ear training
(notes, intervals and chords).

Audio tracks (Control 7): separate a song into vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and the rest, or make an
instrumental without the vocals (with Demucs, which runs on your computer), and change the key of a song.
The separation program is downloaded the first time you use it.

Languages: English (default), Română, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano (Settings tab, Control 8).

Download: [RegatulPartiturilor-Setup.exe](https://github.com/razvan-raftul/RegatulPartiturilor/releases/download/latest/RegatulPartiturilor-Setup.exe).
The program updates itself when it starts.

Formats: MusicXML, MXL, MIDI, ABC, MuseScore, PDF and pictures (recognized with homr and Audiveris, installed with the program).
Made with Python, wxPython and music21.

Program pentru Windows care citește partituri pentru muzicieni nevăzători, accesibil cu NVDA (fostul Regatul Partiturilor).
Limba se alege din fila Setări (Control 8).
Fila Piese audio (Control 7) desparte o piesă în voce, tobe, bas, chitară, pian și restul, face negativul și schimbă tonalitatea.
Fila Player (Control 3) cântă toată piesa; J și L derulează câte 5 secunde, K pornește și pune pauză.
